## ⚡ The 10 Stages of the Crypto Cycle

1. **Smart Money Accumulates** — Price is flat and boring. Whales quietly build positions while nobody is talking about the coin.
2. **Breakout, Early Longs** — Price begins upward momentum. Early technical traders notice, but the broad market is unaware.
3. **KOLs Start Shilling & You Buy In** — Influencers post 100x gem threads. Retail FOMO strikes. You buy in at the dashed entry line.
4. **Retail FOMO at the Top** — Newbies pile in dreaming of quitting their jobs. Smart money unloads bags into retail liquidity.
5. **"Just a Healthy Retracement"** — The first harsh drop. Influencers tell everyone to "buy the dip." You hold.
6. **Dead Cat Bounce** — A brief relief rally offers a last chance to exit near break-even. Hope surges, and retail holds.
7. **You Hold, Down 90%** — The agonizing slow bleed. Liquidity vanishes, balance drops 90%+, and retail enters total despair.
8. **Smart Money Buys Again** — At rock bottom, the same smart money accumulates the next cycle while retail has quit.
9. **Fundamentals Bring It Back** — Real builders and organic protocol revenue recover the price back to your original entry price ($100).
10. **You Sell at Break-even... Then It Pumps Without You!** — After 2 years of agonizing pain, you exit flat at $0 profit just to feel safe... only for the asset to skyrocket into parabolic all-time high price discovery.

## 🛡️ How To Not Be The Exit Liquidity

### 1. Do Your Own Research
> *"Never rely on anyone else in this space. Every KOL has a bag."*
- Check vesting schedules, token unlock calendars, dev commits, and on-chain whale wallet concentration before entering.

### 2. Buy Fundamentals
> *"Pick something you believe is strong and can give a good return."*
- Focus on real fee generation, actual product-market fit, and sticky users over pure marketing hype.

### 3. Set a Realistic Target
> *"Decide your profit goal before you buy, and exit when you hit it."*
- Scale out in tranches (e.g. 25% at 2x, 25% at 3x) and take your initial capital off the table.

### 💡 The Golden Rule
> *"Coin still running after you sold? No worries, you still made money. Wait for a good re-entry, or move on to the next one."*

## 🚀 Features

- **Interactive SVG Curve Animation**: Hand-crafted SVG paths with neon glow, dynamic tracer head, and animated milestones.
- **Real-time Telemetry HUD**: Live tracking of Phase, Simulated Asset Price, $10,000 Portfolio PnL, and Retail Sentiment.
- **Sound Effects Engine**: Built-in zero-dependency Web Audio API synthesizer for pump chimes, dump alerts, and cash-out pings.
- **Full Playback Controls**: Play, Pause, Prev/Next stage, Reset, 0.5x / 1x / 2x speed switcher, and scrubbable 10-stage stepper pills.
- **Self-Audit Quiz**: Test if your trading habits put you at risk of becoming exit liquidity.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Sleek dark fintech terminal aesthetic with instant light mode toggle.
- **Social Sharing**: Pre-formatted Twitter/X sharing and link copier.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript — blazingly fast and works in any browser.
