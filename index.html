🛡️ Survival Playbook

How to not be the exit liquidity

The crypto market is designed to transfer wealth from the impatient and emotional to the disciplined. Here is how you protect yourself.

🔍
Rule 01 • Research

Do your own research

"Never rely on anyone else in this space. Every KOL has a bag."

Actionable Rule: Verify team vesting schedules, token inflation rate, on-chain liquidity locks, and dev repository commits before buying. If an influencer has to scream about it, they are likely looking for your exit liquidity.
💎
Rule 02 • Selection

Buy fundamentals

"Pick something you believe is strong and can give a good return."

Actionable Rule: Look for authentic product-market fit, real on-chain fee generation, active daily users, and sticky utility. If a project has zero product and only hype, it will bleed 99% in a bear market.
🎯
Rule 03 • Execution

Set a realistic target

"Decide your profit goal before you buy, and exit when you hit it."

Actionable Rule: Greed is what turns 5x gains into a 90% loss. Set limit orders in tiers (e.g. scale out 25% at 2x, 25% at 3x, withdraw initial capital). Once your target hits, execute without second-guessing.
✨ The Trader's Mindset
"Coin still running after you sold? No worries, you still made money. Wait for a good re-entry, or move on to the next one."

Selling too early and taking real profit is a win every single time. The market runs 24/7/365 — there will always be another trade, another setup, and another cycle. Protect your principal capital first, and your profits second.
